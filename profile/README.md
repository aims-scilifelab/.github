AiMS is a research lab at KTH and SciLifeLab. We think in terms of **methods** and
**domains** rather than individual projects, and most of our work sits where several of
them meet.

**Methods**
- **Foundation & Generative Models** — large-scale pretraining and generative modeling for images, language and biological data
- **Representation & Multimodal Learning** — shared representations connecting imaging, molecular, cellular and clinical data
- **Robust & Trustworthy AI** — generalization, distribution shift, uncertainty and evaluation
- **Human–AI Decision Making** — when, and how much, AI assistance actually helps an expert decide

**Domains**
- **AI × Medicine** — screening, diagnosis, risk and clinical decision support, validated toward real-world use
- **Cellular & Spatial Biology** — Cell Painting, spatial transcriptomics and high-content cellular readouts
- **Tissue & Pathology** — computational analysis of tissue morphology, microscopy and pathology
- **Biomedical Discovery** — computational biology, phenotype modeling and molecular/cellular applications

**Groups**
- **[Smith Lab](https://aims-scilifelab.github.io/groups/smith-group)** — ML for medical imaging and the life sciences
- **[Lagergren Lab](https://aims-scilifelab.github.io/groups/lagergren-group)** — probabilistic modeling and deep learning for computational biology
- **[Miranda Lab](https://aims-scilifelab.github.io/groups/miranda-group)** — ML for cellular and spatial biology
- **[Taheri Lab](https://aims-scilifelab.github.io/groups/taheri-group)** — ML for biological systems and precision medicine
